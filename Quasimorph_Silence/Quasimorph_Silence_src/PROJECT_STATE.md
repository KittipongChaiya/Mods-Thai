## Decisions

- 2026-09-02 — **One patch, because the API is public.** `CreatureSystem.PropagateNoise`
  is public static and is the single funnel for every noise event in the game — steps,
  doors, gunshots, explosions, deaths. Prefix scales, postfix observes. Everything else
  the mod uses (`GlobalSettings` radii via `Data.Global`, `Creature.MovementState`,
  `MapRenderer.GetCellUnderCursor`, `AIState.ReactsToNoise`, `NoiseType`) is public.
- 2026-09-02 — **Noise is attributed by cell, not by creature.** `PropagateNoise` takes a
  position rather than a source. No two creatures share a cell and every event is raised
  at the acting creature's own cell, so comparing against the player's position is sound.
  This is why enemies are structurally safe: they never match.
- 2026-09-02 — **Only `NoiseType.Step` scales with movement mode.** Applying the
  multiplier to gunshots would be a free silencer.
- 2026-09-02 — **`minimum_radius` floor of 1.** A zero radius means an enemy beside you
  does not hear you open the door in their face, which reads as a bug, not as stealth.
- 2026-09-02 — **The global radii are exposed but default to off.** They are genuinely
  global — every creature reads them — so they are documented as a world setting rather
  than dressed up as a stealth option.
- 2026-09-02 — **Scales go both ways, and up is the interesting direction.** Defaults
  already make running louder than vanilla (`run_noise_scale=1.5`). Paired with Ruthless,
  whose enemies keep hunting after losing you, a loud floor is harder than vanilla.
- 2026-09-02 — **Readout needs no patch**, cloning `MoveStatePanel._actionPointsLeft` the
  way Signals clones the follow button. Falls back to log-only if the field is gone.
- 2026-09-02 — **AP is derived, not read.** `CreatureData` carries `APUsedThisTurn` and
  `BaseActionPoints`, not a remaining figure, and the true maximum depends on movement
  state — so `MoveSystem.GetMaxActionPoints` is asked rather than the base used directly.
